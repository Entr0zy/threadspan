ThreadSpan Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

ThreadSpan is made by ENTR0ZY. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app on iPhone, iPad or Android.

The short version

ThreadSpan does not collect, store or share any of your data. There is no account, no sign-up and no server run by us. Your documents, imported archives and media stay on your device unless you share them yourself.

Your documents and media

Everything you write, every file you import (Word, PDF, text and Markdown) and every photo, video or file you attach is saved only on your device. We can't see any of it.

Photos

If you choose to attach a photo or video, ThreadSpan asks for access to your photo library and copies only the items you pick.

X archives and post links

If you import the archive you downloaded from X, it is read on your device and the original ZIP is kept unchanged. If you import a public post by its link, your phone fetches that public post and its photos and videos directly from X (x.com, cdn.syndication.twimg.com and the image servers it links to) and, for long threads, from threadreaderapp.com. Those services see an ordinary request from your phone, as they would in a browser. Nothing is sent to us.

Opening X

When you choose to post or open a thread in X, ThreadSpan opens X with the text you prepared. What happens there is covered by X's own privacy policy.

Sharing and export

Files you export or share go only where you send them, through your device's own share sheet.

No tracking

ThreadSpan has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind.

Children

ThreadSpan does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete a document or thread inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
