ThreadSpan Support

Something not working, or a question about importing? It helps to say which device you are on and what you were importing. Email entrozy@protonmail.com and you'll get a reply, usually within two days.

Common questions

Which files can I import? Word (.docx), PDF, plain text and Markdown files, from the Files app or any app that can share them.

How do I import my X archive? On X, go to Settings → Your account → Download an archive of your data. When the ZIP arrives, save it to Files, then in ThreadSpan open Threads → + → Choose archive ZIP. Large archives can take a few minutes.

Is my writing backed up? ThreadSpan has no cloud of its own. Export documents (TXT, Markdown, PDF or DOCX) to keep copies elsewhere.

A post link won't import. Only public posts can be imported. Check the post opens in a browser without signing in, then try again.
